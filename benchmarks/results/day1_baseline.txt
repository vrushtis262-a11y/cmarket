Profiling findings:

1. MatchingEngine::rebuild_order_book()
   - Primary observed hotspot.
   - Rebuilds the complete aggregated book after mutations.
   - Scans all active orders.
   - Constructs temporary std::map containers.
   - Constructs temporary PriceLevel vectors.
   - Causes allocation/free activity.

2. Order lookup/storage
   - OrderManager uses vector-based storage.
   - Profiling showed significant work around order management.
   - Inspect lookup/cancellation implementation before optimization.

3. MatchingEngine::find_best_match()
   - Scans active orders to locate the best price/time match.
   - Similar scanning occurs during market-order execution.

Additional candidate:
- Exception-safety backups copy major engine state before mutations.

Scaling observation:
Increasing the workload from 10,000 to 100,000 orders caused
limit-order placement latency to increase from roughly 20 us/op
to roughly 198 us/op.

Day 2 goal:
Reduce allocations and eliminate unnecessary full-book scans/rebuilds
while preserving matching correctness, price-time priority, and
exception safety.
