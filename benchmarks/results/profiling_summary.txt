cmarket Performance Profiling Summary
======================================

Benchmark Environment
---------------------
Build: Release
Workload size: 100,000 operations
Initial active orders: 100,000

Baseline Results
----------------
Order placement:   40,640 ops/sec
Heavy matching:     3,839 ops/sec
Place/cancel mix:   8,868 ops/sec
Place/modify mix:   4,937 ops/sec
Mixed workload:     5,611 ops/sec

Profiling Findings
------------------
Primary hotspot:
_platform_memmove dominated CPU samples during heavy matching.

Root cause:
Active orders are stored in std::vector<LimitOrder>. Removing filled
orders with vector::erase requires shifting subsequent elements,
creating significant memory movement under matching-heavy workloads.

Additional Bottlenecks
----------------------
1. Linear scan in MatchingEngine::find_best_match().
2. Linear lookup in OrderManager::find_order().
3. Linear lookup followed by vector erase in cancel_order().
4. State copies used for strong exception safety in critical paths.

Optimization Opportunities
--------------------------
- Reduce copying and unnecessary allocations in hot paths.
- Improve active-order lookup/removal complexity.
- Reduce repeated full-book scans during matching.
- Preserve price-time priority and existing correctness guarantees.
