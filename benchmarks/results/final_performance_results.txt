Interpretation
--------------
Throughput results are stable across the three measured runs.

The largest improvement relative to the original profiling baseline is
the place/cancel workload, which improved by approximately 134%.

Heavy matching throughput improved by approximately 120%.

Mixed workload throughput improved by approximately 58%.

At 100,000 active orders, placement and cancellation both remain below
approximately 50 microseconds at p50.

Single-order matching remains below approximately 190 microseconds at
p50.

Modify latency remains approximately 219 microseconds at p50.

A 10-level market match across a 100,000-order book requires
approximately 2.0 milliseconds at p50.

Worst-case latency is intentionally not treated as a deterministic
engine bound. The benchmark runs on a general-purpose operating system,
and maximum observed latency is sensitive to scheduler activity and
other system noise. p50, p95, and p99 are therefore the primary latency
metrics.

Performance Evolution
---------------------
The final results reflect the cumulative retained performance work:

- Profiling identified vector movement and rollback copying as major
  hotspots.
- TradeStore rollback copies were replaced with lightweight
  checkpoints.
- Cancellation rollback was reduced from full-state copies to targeted
  restoration.
- Non-crossing placement avoided unnecessary full OrderBook backups.
- Active-order cancellation was changed from stable vector erase to
  swap-with-back removal.
- FIFO execution semantics were preserved by price and sequence number
  rather than physical vector position.

Remaining Bottlenecks
---------------------
Order lookup still performs a linear scan through active orders.

Best-match discovery also scans active orders.

Modify and multi-step matching paths still retain substantial rollback
and state-management costs.

The current results therefore establish a strong final Week 3 baseline
while also identifying clear future optimization opportunities.

Result
------
The performance-engineering phase materially improved cmarket under
matching-heavy, cancellation-heavy, and mixed workloads while
preserving correctness.

164 / 164 tests passed.
