cmarket Allocation Optimization Results
========================================

Optimizations
-------------
1. Removed the unconditional full OrderBook backup from ordinary
   non-crossing limit-order placement.

   Ordinary placements now use targeted rollback for the individual
   order-book adjustment. Full OrderBook rollback remains available
   for first-local-order and crossing/matching paths.

2. Replaced full TradeStore rollback copies with lightweight
   checkpoints.

   Checkpoints preserve:
   - Stored trade count
   - Next trade ID
   - Next execution sequence number

   This avoids copying the entire accumulated trade history during
   matching and modify operations.

3. Removed full OrderBook and OrderManager rollback copies from
   cancel_order().

   Cancellation now stores only the single order being removed.
   If the order-book adjustment fails, the removed order is restored
   directly.

Correctness
-----------
Full test suite passed after the final optimization:

163 / 163 tests passed.

Profiling Baseline
------------------
Order placement:   40,640 ops/sec
Heavy matching:     3,839 ops/sec
Place/cancel mix:   8,868 ops/sec
Place/modify mix:   4,937 ops/sec
Mixed workload:     5,611 ops/sec

Final Throughput Results
------------------------
Three-run average after the retained Day 2 optimizations:

Order placement:   41,336 ops/sec
Heavy matching:     6,722 ops/sec
Place/cancel mix:  14,177 ops/sec
Place/modify mix:   4,723 ops/sec
Mixed workload:     6,668 ops/sec

Comparison With Profiling Baseline
----------------------------------
Order placement:   40,640 -> 41,336 ops/sec   (+1.7%)
Heavy matching:     3,839 ->  6,722 ops/sec  (+75.1%)
Place/cancel mix:   8,868 -> 14,177 ops/sec  (+59.9%)
Place/modify mix:   4,937 ->  4,723 ops/sec   (-4.3%)
Mixed workload:     5,611 ->  6,668 ops/sec  (+18.8%)

Key Findings
------------
The largest improvement came from eliminating full rollback copies
from cancellation. Place/cancel throughput increased from the final
pre-change average of approximately 8,696 ops/sec to approximately
14,177 ops/sec, an improvement of about 63%.

Replacing TradeStore copies with checkpoints substantially improved
heavy matching performance while preserving trade IDs, execution
sequence numbers, and rollback behavior.

The final heavy-matching throughput is approximately 75% higher than
the original profiling baseline.

The place/modify workload remains slightly below the original baseline.
Its remaining hot path still performs full OrderBook and OrderManager
rollback copies and uses vector-based order storage. These costs are
better addressed by the planned data-structure optimization work.

Remaining Full Rollback Copies
------------------------------
Full OrderBook and OrderManager backups remain in:
- execute_market_order()
- modify_order()

These paths perform multi-step matching mutations and are intentionally
left unchanged for now to preserve strong exception safety.

No full TradeStore rollback copies remain.

Result
------
The Day 2 allocation optimizations were retained because they preserve
correctness and materially improve the most allocation-sensitive
workloads.

Further optimization of matching, lookup, cancellation, and modify
behavior will be handled through the data-structure improvements in
the next performance-engineering phase.
